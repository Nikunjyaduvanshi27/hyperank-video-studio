What it is.
Whether your brand is named in the answer a buyer receives when they ask an AI assistant about your category. Not a ranking. Not a crawl. Named in the answer, in the words the buyer reads.
Why it changed.
A buyer used to compare ten links. Now they read one answer and act on it. The shortlist is made before anyone visits a website.
What you can control.
You cannot edit an AI answer. You can edit the sources it is built from, which is the only lever that exists. That process takes weeks, not days.

You can rank first and still not be in the answer

Both layers are live and they reward different work. Ranking is about the page. Being cited is about everything an engine trusts enough to build an answer from, most of which is not your site.

Frequently asked questions

What does hyperank actually do?

It runs the questions your buyers ask across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Grok and Google AI Overviews, stores every answer, tells you what was said about you and whether it was accurate, and shows which sources produced it.

How is this different from an SEO tool?

An SEO tool tells you where a page ranks in a list of links. hyperank tells you what an AI assistant says when someone asks about you, whether it is correct, and who it recommends instead. Different question, different answer, different fix.

Can you actually change what AI says about me?

Not directly, and anyone who tells you otherwise is overselling. AI answers are assembled from sources. You change the sources. hyperank shows you which sources are being used and which ones you are missing, then helps you produce them. That takes weeks, not days.

Do you publish content automatically?

No. Drafts are generated, you review and approve, then it publishes. Fully automated publishing is how brands end up with visible errors on their own blog. We are not building that.

What do I get for free?

One brand check with no signup and no email. You see the result first. Plus 98 free tools.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes, with terms published on one page rather than negotiated. Multi-client accounts, your brand on the reports, your domain.
