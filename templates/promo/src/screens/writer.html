Lumen vs Northbeam: which fits a 20-person agency

A plain comparison of Lumen and Northbeam for agencies of around 20 people: client portal, utilization, reporting and setup effort.

Lumen
vs
NNorthbeam

The short answer

If you run a 20-person agency and bill clients by the hour, Lumen is usually the better fit: client portal, project plans and utilization sit in one tool, and a team can be running in a day. Northbeam earns its place when you manage a large portfolio with a dedicated operations lead.

Who each tool is built for

Lumen is designed around agency work: a project is a client engagement, and the people on it are visible next to their capacity. Northbeam is a general portfolio tool with strong reporting and more configuration to match.

Our recommendation by agency type

  • Under 30 people, hourly tracking: Lumen.
  • Portfolio of 100+ concurrent projects: Northbeam.
  • Clients who want a self-serve view: Lumen.
