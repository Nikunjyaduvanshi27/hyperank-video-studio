# Pitch rules (founder feedback)

For client-facing sales material (e.g. the 2026-10-18 hyperank sales promo), the founder said: "you dont have to stick to app code as a sales representative you have to be lil smart to pitch what user want to listen and its okay if we say it". Lead with outcomes and benefit language; capabilities the team delivers (done-for-you) count; site-listed features like Grok are fine to name.

**Why:** the video is his sales representative; code-literal hedging made the pitch weak.
**How to apply:** keep only the guardrails that protect him in front of prospects: no pricing (his rule for the promo), no fabricated proof (customer names, logos, testimonials, result numbers), no guaranteed outcomes, third-party stats only verified and sourced, no em dashes, never "no signup". Tutorials still show real tool results. See `STYLE-RULES.md`, `STACK.md`.

Update (2026-10-19, promo BRIEF): the app does not run Grok, so the promo never shows Grok even though the site lists it. The promo `templates/promo/BRIEF.md` hard rules win over the line above.
