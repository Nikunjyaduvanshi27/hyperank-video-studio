## 3. Features, exact copy
**02 / How it works: "Three steps, then it runs on its own"**
1. "Build the question set. Your buyers' real questions, not keywords. We generate candidates from your category and competitors, you approve the final set, and it stays yours to edit."
2. "Run it daily across six engines. Every question, every engine, every day. Every answer stored with its timestamp, its engine and the sources it cited."
3. "Fix the sources that decide the answer. See which pages and threads each engine drew on, find the ones you are missing, and produce them. The answer follows over weeks."
- Demo UI strings: "Question set / Today's run / Sources you're missing"; "Candidates generated from your category and competitors."; "Best AI visibility platform for agencies: Tracked"; "Alternatives to Profound: Accept / Reject"; "Day thirty, without anyone logging in".

"The platform: Four things you can do, and the screens that do them"

**03 / What you can see: "Every answer, kept word for word"** (Visibility Intelligence)
"hyperank runs the questions your buyers actually ask across every major AI engine, on a schedule, and keeps every response. Not a score with no receipts. The actual text, the date, the engine, and the sources it cited."
- "Answer archive. Every response from every engine, timestamped and re-runnable."
- "Accuracy flags. What an engine said, checked against what is true."
- "Who got recommended instead. The names appearing in the answers you are absent from."
- Demo rows: "best AI visibility platform" 4/6 mentioned; "alternatives to Profound" 2/6 mentioned, "Not named - Profound recommended instead"; "how to track ChatGPT mentions" 5/6; "AI visibility platform pricing" 3/6, "Quotes a price you retired".
- Demo widgets: Prompts, Mentions, Sentiments, AI Audit (score 74%, issues 18, passed 132, last scan 2 hours ago).

**04 / What you can change: "Fix the sources, not the symptoms"** (Source Graph)
"AI engines do not read your homepage and decide. They assemble answers from the pages, threads and reviews they trust. Change what those sources say and the answer follows."
- "Citation tracking. Capture every source behind every answer, rank the domains by how often they appear, and turn an unanswerable question about AI behaviour into a finite list of pages."
- "Gap analysis. Compare the sources cited about you against those cited about the brands winning your questions, and produce the difference as a list of things to make."
- "Query fan-out. Expand any question into the sub-queries an engine runs behind it, and target the ones that decided the answer rather than the one you can see."

**05 / What you can publish: "Make the sources you are missing"** (Content Engine)
"Every gap the Source Graph finds is a page, a thread or a post that does not exist yet. The Content Engine drafts it from your own material, and a named approver signs off before anything publishes."
- "Drafts from your own material. Generate from your documents, data and approved claims so every piece says what is true about you rather than what is generally true about your category."
- "A review gate that cannot be skipped. Route every draft to a named approver before publication. Nothing publishes on its own."
- "Presence where engines look. Find the Reddit threads and professional posts your product genuinely belongs in, and draft a reply your team reviews before it goes anywhere."
- UI: "Auto-Pilot calendar" (day/week/month; Brand Analysis, Content, Optimization), content types Article / Comparison / Listicle / LinkedIn, statuses Published / Scheduled / Draft, "Publish automation: Autopilot".

**06 / What you can prove: "The traffic AI sends you, separated from the traffic it does not"** (Analytics)
"AI referrals arrive without a keyword and often without a referrer, so most analytics tools fold them into direct traffic and teams conclude nothing changed. We pull them out, show what can be attributed, and mark what cannot instead of assigning it somewhere convenient."
- "AI referral traffic. Sessions by engine, with the pages they landed on and the answers that sent them."
- "Crawler activity. Every visit from GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and Google-Extended, which URLs they fetched and how often. You see the engines reading you before you see the citations."
- "Alongside your search data. Your Google Search Console numbers in the same view, so AI performance is compared to something rather than floating alone."
- Demo UI tabs: AI referrals / Crawler activity / Search Console; "318 sessions unattributable, marked not assigned".

**Engines tracked:** ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Grok, Google AI Overviews (Microsoft Copilot and Meta AI icons also appear on the site).
